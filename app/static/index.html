The metric, explained

A little more
attacking football.

Champagne Index = (goals + assists) ÷ matches

The project’s name for a club’s recorded attacking output per match, in one competition and one season. A goal with an assist contributes twice: this is an attacking-output proxy, not goals scored, xG or an official football rating.

The match denominator comes from Transfermarkt’s MAX(nb_in_group), a team-season proxy. It can differ slightly from the actual fixture count. Completed league seasons give a more meaningful comparison than one-match cup campaigns.

Spending is recorded incoming transfer fees across the whole club-season. Zero can mean free, a loan, or an undisclosed fee. The index and spending describe different aspects of a season; association does not establish causation.
