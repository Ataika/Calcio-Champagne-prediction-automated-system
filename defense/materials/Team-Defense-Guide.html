Проект за одну минуту

Calcio Champagne — воспроизводимая система футбольных данных: она проверяет связь расходов на трансферы с изменением атакующего индекса в следующем сезоне и с итоговым местом. Сравнение добавляет голы за/против, разницу, ранги, реальные удары, удары в створ и угловые, а также описательные Poisson-оценки атаки и обороны. Поля провайдера xG нет; отдельно показана только экспериментальная оценка по ударам в створ, не настоящая xG. Сверка вратарских данных и связь красных карточек с пропущенными сопровождаются покрытием и ограничениями. Лицензия основного набора Transfermarkt остаётся неподтверждённой.

Новые показатели сравнения. GF — забитые голы; GA — пропущенные; GD = GF−GA. Атакующий ранг строится по голам за матч (чем больше, тем выше место), защитный — по пропущенным за матч (чем меньше, тем выше место). Ранг считается отдельно внутри выбранных лиги и сезона; ничьи делят место. xG отсутствует в загруженной схеме, его нельзя выводить из счёта.

Данные и PostgreSQL

Область: Источники → валидация → COPY → схема и SQL

Что знать

  • Какой снимок данных используется: sources.json, закреплённая ревизия и SHA-256; чем загруженный файл отличается от Git LFS pointer.
  • Единица наблюдения: клуб × сезон × соревнование. Отображаемое имя не является ключом.
  • Почему PostgreSQL соответствует фиксированным столбцам и связям; почему COPY быстрее построчных INSERT для этой загрузки.
  • Что делает индекс (team_id, season_name, competition_id), почему он создаётся после COPY и зачем нужен ANALYZE.
  • Опциональность football-data.co.uk, сопоставление названий клубов, проверка счетов и борьба с дубликатами.
  • Лицензия первичного источника пока не подтверждена; публичная скачиваемость не доказывает открытую лицензию.

Английская реплика для репетиции

Our data layer uses eleven pinned CSV tables. We chose PostgreSQL because the records have fixed schemas and are joined by player, club, competition and season. The loader validates headers, uses COPY for bulk import, and builds indices afterwards. We aggregate by canonical identifiers so name aliases do not duplicate observations. An optional match source adds actual scorelines and standings. Invalid or duplicate matches are handled explicitly. The source is publicly downloadable, but its licence is still unconfirmed; we do not claim that this requirement is resolved.

Передача слова: “With the data stored consistently, the next step is to define a valid season-to-season target and evaluate it.”

Аналитика и модель

Область: Индекс → признаки → target → baseline → ограничения

Что знать

  • Формула Champagne Index; почему goal + assist может учитывать одно взятие ворот дважды и не является xG.
  • Target = индекс следующего последовательного сезона минус текущий; минимум 30 матчей для топ-5 лиг и 5 для прочих соревнований.
  • Пять признаков: текущий индекс, общие расходы, расходы на атаку, средний возраст, изменение средней рыночной стоимости.
  • Как устраняется неверный лаг: уникальный клуб-сезон, точный предыдущий год, затем присоединение соревнований.
  • Хронологический holdout: ранние сезоны обучают, поздние тестируют; baseline предсказывает нулевое изменение.
  • R² = 0.127 не означает 87% или 12.7% точности. MAE 0.4856 против 0.5114 показывает небольшое улучшение.
  • Lab использует отдельную модель на всех допустимых данных и не доказывает причинный эффект изменения бюджета.
  • The season comparison also shows score-derived goals for/against/difference and per-match attack/defense ranks. These are separate from the Transfermarkt-based Champagne Index.
  • The defensive-spend follow-up correlates defender fees with next-season change in goalkeeper-source goals conceded per match: r=0.0040, p=0.427, n=38,531; no useful linear signal.

Что показать

  1. Показать текущие r, p, n и MAE на странице Project; назвать единицы и выборку каждого показателя.
  2. Объяснить, почему 23/24 → 25/26 не является корректной парой, даже если shift(-1) её возвращает.
  3. Поменять один ввод в Lab, вернуть baseline и объяснить, что измеряется сравнением сценариев.

Английская реплика для репетиции

We define attacking output as recorded goals plus assists per source match. The target is the change in that index in the next consecutive season. We remove incomplete season pairs and construct lagged squad values before joining competitions. Evaluation uses earlier seasons for training and later seasons for testing. The RandomForest has a test MAE of about 0.486, compared with 0.511 for predicting no change: an improvement of roughly five percent. This is a modest signal. The comparison also separates the Transfermarkt attacking index from completed league scores: goals for, goals against, their difference, and per-match attack and defense ranks. A defensive-spend follow-up also has almost zero association with next-season conceded-goal change (r=0.004, p=0.427; n=38,531). The Lab uses a separate all-data model for retrospective exploration. Moving a budget slider changes a model input; it does not establish what a transfer would cause.

Передача слова: “The application makes those distinctions visible and lets us inspect the evidence for individual clubs.”

Практика

  1. Рассчитай (76 + 70) / 38 и объясни, почему ответ 3.84 не равен голам за матч.
  2. Посчитай (0.511364 − 0.485593) / 0.511364 ≈ 5.04% и назови правильную интерпретацию.
  3. Различи три вопроса: корреляция в текущем сезоне, изменение в следующем сезоне и причинный эффект покупки игрока.

  3. Главное число произноси как сравнение, а не как достижение: MAE 0.4856 против baseline 0.5114 — улучшение около 5%.
  4. Сразу объясни, что R² = 0.127 не доля правильных прогнозов. Это самый вероятный вопрос по твоей части.
  5. Если спросят про Lab: отдельная модель на всех данных для исследования, её собственная подгонка не является независимым тестом.

Английская реплика для репетиции

Docker Compose coordinates four core services. The database must be healthy, loading and processing must finish successfully, and then the application starts. Source files, the model and measurement results use explicit mounts. We measured timing, observed memory, disk footprint and growth in separate experiments, each repeated three times. Complete cached-source startup averaged 31.10 seconds on an Apple M3 Pro with 18 GiB RAM. The selective index lookup improved by about 145 times, but that result applies to that query and sample. Caches were retained, image builds were excluded and brief memory peaks may have been missed.

Передача слова: “Taken together, the system is reproducible on the measured setup, while the data and model limitations remain explicit.”

Вопросы экзаменатора

Сначала ответь вслух коротко, затем объясни основание и ограничение.

nedalozanovska

Why PostgreSQL rather than MongoDB?

Почему PostgreSQL?

The source tables have fixed columns and relational links by player, club, season and competition. PostgreSQL supports those joins, native COPY and indexed lookups with a clear schema. A different database could work, but this choice follows the data shape.

nedalozanovska

What identifies one observation?

Что является ключом строки?

For the analytical output it is club ID, season and competition ID. A club’s display name is a label, not its identity. We aggregate canonical keys to prevent alias names from creating duplicate observations.

nedalozanovska

Why build indices after loading?

Почему индексы после COPY?

Maintaining indices during a bulk load adds work to every inserted row. We import with COPY, then build the required indices and ANALYZE the updated tables so the next pipeline has current planner statistics.

nedalozanovska

Is your source openly licensed?

Подтверждена ли лицензия?

We verified the pinned source revision, headers and checksums, but did not establish an explicit upstream licence. We document this as unresolved and exclude raw data from the repository. Public downloadability alone is insufficient.

nedalozanovska

What happens if a download is interrupted?

Что будет при обрыве загрузки?

The downloader writes a temporary file and installs it atomically only after checksum and header validation. An interrupted or corrupt download is not accepted as a complete source. Tests exercise these failures using fake HTTP responses.

nedalozanovska

How do you handle duplicate match rows?

Как обрабатываются дубли матчей?

Identical league/season/date/home/away fixtures are imported once. Conflicting scores for the same fixture stop replacement before stored match rows are changed. Missing optional input clears old enrichment on the next loader run.

lilllaz

Is Champagne Index an official metric or xG?

Это официальный индекс или xG?

No. It is this project’s proxy: recorded goals plus assists divided by source matches. A goal with an assist contributes twice. We explain the denominator and do not present it as xG or an official rating.

lilllaz

What exactly does the model predict?

Что именно предсказывает модель?

The next consecutive season’s Champagne Index minus the current index. It does not predict a match score, a calibrated league position or the causal return from buying a player.

lilllaz

Why reject gaps between seasons?

Почему нельзя брать просто следующую строку?

A club may leave a competition and return years later. shift(-1) gives the next recorded row, which is not necessarily next year. We explicitly require a one-year gap and adequate coverage in both seasons.

lilllaz

Why use a temporal split?

Зачем хронологический holdout?

A random row split mixes earlier and later seasons and can make historical evaluation overly optimistic. We train on earlier seasons and test on later ones, keeping observations from the same season together.

lilllaz

How much better is the model than a baseline?

Насколько модель лучше baseline?

On the same 5,585 held-out rows, MAE is about 0.4856 versus 0.5114 for predicting no change. That is about a five percent reduction in MAE, a modest gain. R-squared is about 0.127, not a percentage of correct predictions.

lilllaz

Can you conclude that buying attackers improves performance?

Можно ли утверждать причинный эффект?

No. These are retrospective observational data with confounding and source limitations. A slider changes a model input and shows a learned association, not the outcome of a controlled transfer intervention.

lilllaz

Why is the Lab model different from the evaluation model?

Почему две обученные модели?

The evaluation model uses only earlier training data and is scored on held-out later seasons. A separate model is refitted on all eligible data for exploration. Its own training fit is not claimed as independent test performance.

lilllaz

Why did the index-position correlation change after the audit?

Почему изменилась корреляция?

The earlier result mixed a partial 25/26 attacking snapshot with full final standings. We now require adequate comparable coverage. The corrected same-season association is about minus 0.793 over 388 observations.

jacoposcaccianoce

Why have an API instead of plotting raw tables directly?

Зачем API?

The API exposes small prepared outputs under an explicit league and season. This avoids moving millions of raw rows to the browser, keeps SQL and validation on the server and separates presentation from processing.

jacoposcaccianoce

How are head-to-head goals calculated?

Как считаются голы друг другу?

For each completed meeting, the selected club’s perspective determines whether the home or away score is goals for. Reversing the compared clubs swaps their totals. Wins, draws, losses and points are derived from those actual scores.

jacoposcaccianoce

Does no recorded match mean a 0–0 draw?

Нет данных — это 0–0?

No. No coverage, no recorded meetings and a completed 0–0 draw are different states. The API reports enrichment availability and the interface shows an explicit message instead of inventing a score.

jacoposcaccianoce

What prevents invalid scenario inputs?

Как защищены входы Lab?

The API rejects non-finite numbers, checks bounds and requires attacking spend not to exceed total spend. The browser also clamps the sliders. Server-side validation remains necessary even when the UI enforces the same rule.

jacoposcaccianoce

What do HTTP 422, 404 and 503 mean here?

Что означают ошибки API?

422 indicates invalid input, such as selecting the same club twice. 404 means the requested club-season scope is unavailable. 503 indicates temporary data/model unavailability or an incomplete application output state.

jacoposcaccianoce

What do your tests prove?

Что доказывают тесты?

Regression tests cover known failure cases, live checks validate the prepared outputs and API contracts, and browser checks exercise actual flows and mobile layout. They provide scoped evidence, not a guarantee that every possible bug is absent.

Ataika

What starts when you run Docker Compose?

Что запускает Compose?

The database becomes healthy, then the loader must finish successfully, then the processing pipeline must finish, and finally the application starts. The measurement service is on demand and the legacy interface uses an optional profile.

Ataika

What does the 31.10-second measurement include?

Что входит в 31.10 секунды?

A mean over three complete starts with prepared source files and existing images: containers are recreated, sources reimported and the model retrained until the API is ready. Network acquisition and image builds are excluded and caches are not flushed.

Ataika

Does the index make the whole application 145 times faster?

Всё приложение быстрее в 145 раз?

No. The measured gain is for 300 identical selective club lookups on the raw performance table, comparing index conditions. The frontend reads prepared outputs and a full GROUP BY has a different access pattern.

Ataika

Is your memory number a guaranteed peak?

Это гарантированный пик RAM?

It is the largest observed Docker working-set sample during three runs, sampled at roughly two-second intervals. Brief peaks may be missed. We report the method rather than label it a continuous or hardware-independent maximum.

Ataika

Why did four times as many rows not give exactly four times the query time?

Почему масштабирование не точно линейное?

Fixed costs, query plans, indexing, caches and hardware influence scaling. In this experiment four times the largest-table rows gave about 4.40 times the loading time and 3.35 times the aggregation time. That does not define a general law.

Ataika

What remains before a group submission?

Что ещё нужно для сдачи?

Confirm actual team membership and each person’s contribution, run an independent second-laptop setup, resolve the source licence, rehearse the presentation and record a backup demo. Equal author email counts alone do not demonstrate real group participation.

What is the main result in one sentence?

Главный результат одним предложением?

The system is reproducible on the measured setup, while historical transfer spending alone has almost no linear relationship with next-season index change and the richer model adds only modest predictive value.

Все

What is the most important limitation?

Главное ограничение?

The data are retrospective and unevenly covered, the index is a proxy, and associations are not causal effects. Source licence and independent team/laptop validation also remain explicit unresolved requirements.

Все

How do goals for, goals against and goal difference work in the comparison?

Как в сравнении рассчитываются забитые, пропущенные голы и разница?

GF and GA are season totals rebuilt from completed home/away match scores. GD is GF − GA. They come from the optional football-data.co.uk match enrichment, not from the Champagne Index player totals.

Все

How are attacking and defensive ranks calculated?

Как рассчитываются атакующий и защитный рейтинги?

Within one league and season, attack rank orders goals scored per match from highest to lowest; defense rank orders goals conceded per match from lowest to highest. SQL RANK gives tied rates the same rank and leaves a gap after ties. They describe scores, not tactics or chance quality.

Все

Do we have xG or can we estimate it?

Есть ли в данных xG или можем ли мы его оценить?

The source has no provider xG field. The comparison instead shows observed shots and shots on target. A separate estimate multiplies shots on target by the league-season average goals-per-shot-on-target rate; it is an experimental volume proxy with no shot-location or chance-quality input, not xG.

Все

What is the difference between the Champagne Index and attack rank?

Чем атакующий рейтинг отличается от Champagne Index?

The Champagne Index is recorded player goals plus assists divided by a Transfermarkt matches proxy for a club-season. Attack rank uses actual completed league scorelines, sums team goals and ranks goals per match within the league-season. Their sources, coverage and units differ.

Все

How did you check the goalkeeper defensive totals?

Как вы проверили вратарские показатели по пропущенным?

We compared Transfermarkt goalkeeper goals-conceded totals with the independent match-result totals on 388 adequately covered top-five club-seasons. They match exactly in 366 (94.3%). Clean sheets match only 269 (69.3%), so the scoreline source remains the basis of league ranks.

Все

What does the defensive-spend analysis show?

Что показывает анализ расходов на защитников?

The Pearson correlation between defender transfer fees and next-season change in the goalkeeper-source conceded-goal rate is r=0.004047, p=0.426997, n=38,531. The sample does not support a useful linear association. It is descriptive, not causal. A separate match-level check finds teams with a red card concede 0.341 more goals on average in the same match, but match context confounds that association.

Все

Why keep the player-source defensive values separate from scoreboard goals?

Почему defensive-показатели по игрокам отделены от итогов матчей?

The sources use different records and denominators. Goalkeeper conceded totals agree with scorelines in 94.3% of adequately covered seasons, while clean sheets agree in 69.3%. The UI therefore uses match scores for GF, GA, GD and ranks; player-source totals remain a quality cross-check. Shots and corners are also observed match statistics, not chance-quality measures.

Все

Is the shot-based estimate the same as xG?

Оценка по ударам — это xG?

No. It multiplies a team’s shots on target by the league-season average goals per shot on target. It has no shot-location, distance, body-part, pressure or goalkeeper-position input. It is a rough volume benchmark and must be called an SOT-based proxy, not provider xG.

Все

What does the Poisson attack/defense rank tell us?

Что показывают Poisson-ранги атаки и обороны?

The model fits attack, defense and a shared home-advantage term to the selected league-season scores. Larger fitted attack and defense parameters mean stronger estimated attack and defense. Its ranks are descriptive and in-sample; the separate chronological RandomForest evaluation is the project’s held-out prediction check.

Все

Did the added defensive features improve the model?

Улучшили ли защитные признаки модель?

On the same chronological split, R² moves from 0.1271 with the five-feature baseline to 0.1502 after adding goalkeeper conceded rate, discipline and net spend; MAE moves from 0.4856 to 0.4788 on 5,585 held-out rows. The gain is modest, and it does not isolate a causal effect of defense.

Все

Do red cards cause more goals to be conceded?

Красные карточки приводят к большему числу пропущенных?

The local sample shows a same-match difference of +0.341 conceded goals per team-match (1,619 sent-off vs 16,195 others; Welch p < 0.0001). This is observational: stronger opponents, match state and timing can affect both red cards and goals. It is not a causal estimate.

Как отвечать на вопросы

  • Отвечай тремя шагами: короткий ответ одним предложением → основание (файл или число) → ограничение.
  • Каждое число называй с выборкой и единицей: «r = −0.79 на 388 клуб-сезонах в одном сезоне», а не «корреляция высокая».
  • Не знаешь ответа — скажи, где он лежит, и не придумывай: «это в docs/MEASUREMENTS.md, метод измерения памяти описан там».
  • Вопрос не по твоей части — передай одной фразой владельцу направления, не пересказывай чужую зону приблизительно.
  • Никогда не превращай связь в причину: говори accompanies, is associated with, а не causes или improves.
  • Если спросили про слабое место — отвечай прямо и первым. Признанное ограничение выглядит сильнее, чем защищаемая неточность.
